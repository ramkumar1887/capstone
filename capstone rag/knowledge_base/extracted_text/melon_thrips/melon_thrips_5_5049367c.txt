Symptoms and How to Find Thrips
Small thrips seen with the naked eye (the rod-shaped object in the center)
Thrips are also known as thrips. They are about 1-2 mm long and their body colors are brown, yellow, or black.
They are very small and move quickly, making them difficult to find.
The thrips that mainly cause problems in eggplant cultivation are the western flower thrips, melon thrips, flower thrips, and onion thrips.
Thrips infest leaves, suck nutrients from them to hinder growth, and create white, scratch-like spots on the leaves.
Also, melon thrips suck nutrients from the fruit, which damages the fruit and makes it look bad.
In addition, because thrips transmit viruses, eggplants can become infected with viral diseases and wither.

Eggplant in pesticide regulations
There are various types of eggplants depending on the shape of the fruit. In pesticide regulations, medium-long eggplants, long eggplants, water eggplants, and round eggplants are classified as "eggplant." Therefore, please follow the information for eggplant regarding pesticide usage methods.
Please read the usage precautions before using pesticides
When using pesticides, please check the information on the package or the manufacturer's website and follow the usage instructions. While I strive for accuracy, pesticide information often changes, so please check the latest information from the manufacturer.
Granular pesticides [For prevention when planting seedlings]
Granular pesticides can be used by putting the powder directly into the planting hole when planting seedlings. By absorbing the pesticide ingredients through the roots, you can prevent pests. To prevent thrips, use granular pesticides when planting seedlings.
Iroiro na Shokubutsu Tsuyoshi (Dinotefuran)
If you want to prevent aphids, leaf miners, whiteflies, and thrips on eggplants, Iroiro na Shokubutsu Tsuyoshi (granule type) is recommended. The active ingredient is dinotefuran. You can use it once when planting seedlings and twice more by the day before harvest during the growing season, for a total of three times, allowing for long-term pest protection.

Can Iroiro na Shokubutsu Tsuyoshi and Magamp D be used together?
The active ingredient of Iroiro na Shokubutsu Tsuyoshi is "dinotefuran." The active ingredient of Magamp D is also "dinotefuran." Therefore,
you must be careful about the number of times you use the active ingredient dinotefuran.
The number of uses for dinotefuran is limited to 5 times, with soil mixing and drenching limited to a total of 1 time, base-of-plant application during the seedling stage limited to 1 time, soil mixing at the time of planting limited to 1 time, and spraying and base-of-plant application after planting limited to a total of 2 times.
Therefore, you cannot "use Iroiro na Shokubutsu Tsuyoshi 3 times and Magamp D 3 times (a total of 6 times)."
The maximum number of uses is a total of 3 times, using either one once at the time of planting and twice during the early growth stage.

Spray Bottle Insecticides [For Planter Cultivation]
Spray bottle insecticides are convenient because they can be used as-is without diluting with water. They are recommended for planter cultivation with a small number of plants. However, there are few spray bottle pesticides that are effective against thrips.

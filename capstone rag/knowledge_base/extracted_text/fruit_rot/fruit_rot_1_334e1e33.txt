Natural Disease Management Strategies
Treat your seeds with neem oil or a copper fungicide before planting to deter fungal pathogens.
Don't plant brinjal in the same spot year after year. Rotate your crops to disrupt disease cycles.
Provide adequate spacing between brinjal plants to ensure good air circulation, which discourages fungal diseases.
Water deeply and less frequently, avoiding wetting the foliage. This helps prevent fungal spores from spreading.
Apply a layer of organic mulch around your brinjal plants to suppress weeds, retain moisture, and regulate soil temperature.
Consider planting companion crops like marigolds or basil near your brinjal to deter pests and potentially suppress certain diseases.
The Scouting Spree: Early Detection is Key
Give those eggplant babies a thorough check-up for any sneaky diseases! Catching them early can be a game-changer in keeping your plant party disease-free. Keep an eye out for droopy leaves, yellowing, mysterious spots, or any stems that seem a bit too soggy.
